# Vintage botanical shop UI

Selected direction: dark forest green, walnut workbench, aged-paper labels and restrained brass detail. This is an evolution of the existing first greenhouse layout. No external screenshots or game assets are included in the game.

## Visual references
- Strange Horticulture: https://store.steampowered.com/app/1574580/Strange_Horticulture/ — botanical specimens, aged label stock, wooden material organisation.
- Botany Manor: https://whitethorngames.com/botany-manor — concentrated natural light and readable planting objects.
- Viridi: https://store.steampowered.com/app/375950/Viridi/ — plant-focused composition and quiet care interactions.

## Changes
Dark green panelled room; walnut material cabinet; cream labels with serif typography; brass accents; consistently placed material selection on the left and terrarium on the right. The active stage, noninteractive progress track and confirmation control form one vertical group. Visible focus states, selected-item borders and a jar placement target improve affordance. Reduced-motion settings are retained. No changes to stage rules, resident positions or watering growth.


Object-based handoff revision: stage headings, numbered progress and Finish controls have been removed. Soil/creatures occupy the left side; plants/watering occupy the right. Upcoming materials appear after successful placement while current materials stay usable. Picking up the next material hides the previous group. Only specimen information labels remain visible.
